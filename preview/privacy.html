PILOT INFORMATION

Privacy information

This guide describes the current pilot’s data features. School participation, retention periods and formal policy details must be confirmed before a school enrolls students.

What the app stores

Guest practice, learning preferences and character choices can be stored in your browser. Account features use Firebase for sign-in and saved learning records, including completed figures, XP and activity days. School features store memberships, class invitations, assignments, results and support messages. Pilot applications include the details entered in the application form.

Who can see learning records

School access depends on membership and role. A linked parent can see the progress shared through the family workspace. The student can disconnect that family link. Removing a link or school membership is not the same as deleting an account or its records.

Email, payments and analytics

Firebase supports account services, Resend delivers branded service emails, and Stripe handles the payment flow when enabled. Optional Google Analytics on the public marketing website runs after you allow it. You can change that choice using Analytics preferences on the homepage. The learner and staff workspaces do not install that marketing analytics script.

Your choices and questions

You can use guest practice without creating an account. New independent student registration is currently limited to ages 13 and older. A school invitation is not, by itself, evidence that the school’s privacy and consent arrangements are complete.

For questions about access, corrections, export or deletion, contact support@reckoningfigures.com. Do not email passwords or payment-card details. There is no automatic account-deletion control in the current app; a request needs to be reviewed and handled separately.
