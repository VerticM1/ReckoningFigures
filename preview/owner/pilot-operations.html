Preparation guide. Backups and email delivery have not been activated or verified by this page. Keep actual school details, incident logs and recovery evidence in restricted storage.

Before inviting students

  1. Confirm the pilot teacher, school lead, dates, approved student group and devices.
  2. Agree consent, approved data fields, retention and deletion/export handling with the school.
  3. Approve the selected lessons with an educator and complete a test assignment from invitation through the teacher report.
  4. Name the support responder and backup contact. Agree session hours and a response target with the school; no response-time guarantee is currently published.
  5. Complete the backup and restore drill below before relying on recovery.

Each pilot session

  1. Before: check school license, seats, assignment and support inbox.
  2. During: confirm students entered the correct class and see “Completion saved to your class”. Pause on a material answer error, missing results or unexpected access.
  3. After: review the report and support inbox. Ask the teacher what confused students, what helped, and whether they can solve a fresh problem independently.

Students ask their teacher for help. Staff use School support. Replies are read inside the app; email notifications are not connected.

When something breaks

  1. Record time, affected feature, device/browser and reproduction steps. Never put passwords or student data in public issues.
  2. For exposed data, wrong-class access or lost results, stop the affected session and contact the owner. Use school pause when appropriate; it blocks new practice but does not delete records or terminate every existing session.
  3. Preserve evidence privately. Identify the known-good app commit and matching rule version. Verify a fix or rollback in a test environment first.
  4. Reopen only after access checks and a fresh assignment save/report test pass. Explain the outcome to the school through its agreed contact route.

Backup and restore drill

  1. Have the project administrator confirm Firebase/Google Cloud access, billing, data location and recovery responsibility.
  2. Configure a Firestore backup schedule and retention appropriate to the school agreement. Record the successful backup resource, timestamp and coverage privately.
  3. Restore a selected backup into a new isolated test database. Never delete or overwrite production to run a drill. Keep test data access restricted.
  4. Verify school records, memberships, classes, assignments, results and support threads. Test access rules and indexes separately before running the app against the restored data.
  5. Firestore backup does not back up Firebase Authentication users. Establish a separate restricted Auth export/recovery procedure, including handling of password hashes and provider configuration. Never commit exports to GitHub.
  6. Record restore duration, data age, checks, operator and result. Agree acceptable recovery time/data loss with the school. Mark the drill complete only after a successful test.

Teacher feedback prompts

  • Where did a student or teacher get stuck?
  • Which figure and step? What did you expect, and what happened?
  • Was the explanation useful? Could the student solve a new example afterward?
  • What should we fix before the next session?

Submit through School support. Use fictional examples or figure numbers rather than student names.
