PILOT INFORMATION

Web pilot guide

A practical guide to the early web pilot. This page is not a finalized purchase agreement.

Learning in the pilot

Reckoning Figures currently offers Algebra 1 practice. Pre-Algebra and future courses are not available yet. Lessons and explanations are still being reviewed. Tell us the figure number and what seemed wrong if you find a problem.

Completion and XP record app activity; they are not a certification of independent mastery. The practice leaderboard uses simulated opponents.

Accounts and school access

Keep passwords and private invitation links to yourself. Use your own account. A teacher or administrator needs the correct school invitation and membership. Independent students under 13 cannot register through the current signup flow. Schools must confirm participation and data-handling arrangements before inviting students.

Payments

Public purchases are not open yet. Saving a plan selection does not charge you or unlock Premium. Any future checkout must state the plan, price and billing period. Refund terms and the final purchase agreement must be established before paid launch.

Help and feedback

School staff can use School support. Independent learners and families can email support@reckoningfigures.com. Include the affected page or figure and a description, without student records, passwords or card details.
