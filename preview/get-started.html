School access starts with an invitation. Teachers and school administrators have their own school workspace. The owner workspace is for platform management.

Teacher

Join your school.

  1. Open the staff invitation link your administrator shares.
  2. Create an account or sign in with the exact invited email address.
  3. If prompted, choose Send verification email. Open the email, return to your invitation, and choose I’ve verified my email.
  4. Choose Join school, then Open classes. Bookmark your school link for future sign-ins.
  5. Open or create your class. Choose Invite students, share the class link, then use Assign practice and the assignment report.

Missing your link? Ask your school administrator to resend it. Creating an account alone does not grant school access.

School administrator

Set up your team.

  1. The platform owner creates the school and invites you as an administrator.
  2. Accept your invitation with the matching verified email address.
  3. In your school workspace, choose Invite staff, enter the teacher’s email and create the invitation.
  4. Copy and share the link. Invitation emails are not sent automatically.
  5. Open classes, select an active teacher by name, and create the class. Review members and available seats in the school workspace.

Staff invitation links expire after seven days and can be revoked. Only the platform owner invites administrators.

Student

Your teacher’s link takes you to class.

  1. Open the class invitation link your teacher shares.
  2. Enter your name and create an account, or sign in to your existing account.
  3. With an automatic invitation, you enter the class if a seat is available. With an approval invitation, wait for your teacher to approve the request.
  4. Open your assignment and complete a figure. Wait for Completion saved to your class. If saving fails, use the retry button.

You do not need to find or type a StudentUID. School assignments do not require a separate individual Premium purchase.

If you get stuck

I created an account but can’t see my school.

Return to your staff invitation and finish Join school. The email must match the invitation. If you already joined, open the school link you bookmarked or ask your administrator for it. The generic school page cannot currently find all your schools for you.

The verification email has not arrived.

Check the email shown on the verification screen and your spam folder. Avoid repeatedly sending requests. If it still does not arrive, ask the administrator to raise a support request so the owner can check Firebase email settings and delivery.

I need help with an assignment.

Teachers and administrators can open School support from the school workspace. Include the figure number, device/browser and what happened. Do not include student names, passwords or private student information. Students should ask their teacher.

Platform owner? Open owner workspace. This guide explains current behavior; it is not a sign-up form or a promise that email delivery has been verified.
