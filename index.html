A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

What does Reckoning Figures cover?

The learning experience focuses on Algebra 1. Visit the curriculum page for the current topic coverage and review information.

How do students join a class?

Students open the invitation link their teacher shares, enter their name and create an account or sign in. An automatic invitation enrolls them if a seat is available; an approval invitation waits for the teacher’s approval. No StudentUID needs to be entered.

Where do teachers and administrators sign in?

Use the school workspace. First-time staff need to accept their invitation using the matching email address. The access guide walks through each step.

Do I need to download an app?

You can explore Reckoning Figures in your browser. This website does not require an App Store download.
