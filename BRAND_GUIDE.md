# Reckoning Figures brand

Promise: Find your spark. Mathematics makes things possible.

Keep the existing transparent lightning bolt and the Reckoning Figures wordmark together on entry pages. Use Fredoka for the wordmark and short display headings; Nunito for body text, controls, tables and labels. Both fonts are bundled locally.

Palette: midnight #101719, panel #18262d, electric cyan #65d8f0, gold #ffd15c, ivory #f1f0e8. Cyan identifies primary actions; gold adds emphasis. Never rely on color alone for status.

Original illustration family: rounded geometric bridges, orbits, waves, mechanisms and patterns. Start with preview/assets/math-world.svg. No mascots, faces, emojis, copied Duolingo artwork or decorative equations presented as instruction. Illustrations are conceptual, not engineering diagrams.

Learner and public pages can be expressive. Staff workspaces use the same identity with smaller accents and artwork confined to entry or empty states. Keep working tables and forms focused. Preserve keyboard focus, descriptive labels, 44px primary controls, mobile layouts and reduced-motion preferences.

Voice: warm, direct, specific. Explain the next action. Do not promise mastery, automated email, access or features that are not connected.

For future emails: bolt + wordmark, clear subject, one primary action and a support route. Email delivery and branded sender setup remain pending; this guide does not configure them.

Reference principles: Duolingo official identity typography, imagery and shape-language guidance. Our logo, palette and math artwork remain Reckoning Figures originals.

## Revised direction
Use dimensional inventions and imaginative architecture (brand-invention.webp and brand-architecture.webp) instead of the rejected flat math-world.svg illustration. Artwork is conceptual, not an accurate mechanism or construction guide. Original generated previews were converted to WebP for delivery without changing their composition.
